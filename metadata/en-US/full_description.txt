A lightweight digital-audio-player HOME interface for the Sony NW-ZX1. It browses the music library - playlists, artists, albums, songs - in its own period Sony-style LCD interface, reading the firmware's media database directly and starting playback on the original Sony player without leaving the launcher. The LCD shows the playing track with its audio format, play state, battery, and album art shaded like a period STN panel, and a searchable program index lists every installed application. Play, pause, skip and volume belong to the ZX1's physical keys; holding the album art brings up a hidden strip for seeking within the track. The interface uses platform views and remains compatible with Android 4.1.
